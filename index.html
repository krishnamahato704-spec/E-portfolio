03 · Why History

From memorisation to understanding

My study of History moved from memorising events to asking why they happened, how we know, and what they mean for people in different contexts. Working with sources such as Kautilya's Arthashastra and accounts of Partition strengthened my interest in teaching History as evidence-based inquiry that connects past and present.

04 · Teaching Philosophy

Nurturing the whole person

🌟
Holistic Development

I plan for the whole learner: subject knowledge, confidence, curiosity, and respectful participation. Classroom interactions should help learners grow as people as well as historians.

🔍
Seeing Potential

I look for strengths as well as gaps, then use clear feedback and encouragement to help learners take the next step toward independent thought.

🛤️
Differentiation

Differentiation means offering different routes—scaffolded prompts, visual sources, discussion, or deeper comparison—toward the same meaningful understanding.

A practical cycle guides my teaching: notice what learners can already do, make the next idea visible, invite them to practise, and use evidence from their responses to plan the next step.

Encouragement should preserve confidence while feedback stays specific enough to improve the work.

05 · Subject Expertise

Teaching History as a discipline of the mind

Beyond Dates & Events

True historical learning asks students to question—Why did this happen? To compare—How was this different elsewhere? To interpret—What did this mean to those who lived it? I want my students to connect the past with the present, to reflect on what they have understood, and to reconsider their assumptions.

❓ Questioning 🔍 Comparison 💭 Interpretation 📜 Primary sources

Inquiry & Differentiation

I intend to teach History through inquiry, discussion, and creative engagement—using timelines, visual sources, and primary documents to make the past tangible. For academically strong learners, I offer deeper research and comparative analysis. For creatively inclined learners, I use paintings, photographs, and storytelling to bring history to life. Every student reaches the same destination through a path suited to them.
