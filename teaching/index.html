Teaching philosophy

Start with the learner.

01

Inquiry before recall

I begin with a question, source or puzzle so that dates and events become evidence in an explanation—not isolated facts.

02

Different routes, shared depth

Scaffolded prompts, visual sources, discussion and deeper comparison help learners reach the same meaningful understanding.

03

Assessment that changes teaching

Short checks, source-based tasks and specific feedback reveal what learners understand and what the next lesson must address.

Rabindranath Tagore

Room for curiosity.

Holistic education and creative learning remind me to keep History connected to the whole learner.

Lev Vygotsky

Support toward independence.

Dialogue, guided source work and peer interaction help learners move toward independent historical reasoning.
