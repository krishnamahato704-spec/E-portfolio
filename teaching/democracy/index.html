STAGE 01

Context & intention

The shared learning intention

Lesson Plan No. 6 is an Amity University school-practicum plan for Social Science, Class IX-B, session 2026–27. It is dated 25 July 2026 and plans a 40-minute face-to-face lesson.

The objectives are to recall the meaning and features of democracy, distinguish forms of government, and explain the role of elections. The plan also identifies respect for different opinions and responsible citizenship.

STAGE 02

Prior knowledge

Begin with what learners know

The opening questions ask learners to define democracy, identify Abraham Lincoln’s familiar definition, and recall monarchy, oligarchy and dictatorship. The plan connects previous learning about Sabha and Samiti to participation.

These are planned diagnostic questions. The listed student answers are expected responses, not a record of what a class actually said.

STAGE 03

Explanation & discussion

Use visuals and questions

A concept diagram supports explanation of direct and indirect democracy and parliamentary and presidential systems. India and the United States provide a comparison.

Election and protest photographs support discussion of representation, accountability and citizen participation. The plan asks why elections recur and whether peaceful protest is necessary in democracy, allowing different viewpoints.

STAGE 04

Learner activity & support

Oral and written ways to respond

Learners are asked to recall, discuss, explain and complete written responses. Resources include a quotation visual, concept diagram, election and protest photographs, blackboard work and response sheets.

These provide more than one way to encounter and explain the idea. The document does not specify differentiated tasks, individual learning needs or targeted scaffolds. Documenting those choices is a next step in developing this plan.

STAGE 05

Assessment

Check for understanding

Diagnostic
Recall democracy and other forms of government before the new explanation.
Guided practice
Define democracy, name three forms of government, and explain elections.
Independent practice
Explain the role of protest in ensuring accountability.
Evaluation
Give reasons for comparing democracy favourably with other forms of government.

The file contains prompts, not completed response sheets, marking criteria or feedback. It supports a review of assessment planning; it does not establish student progress.

STAGE 06

Reflection & next evidence

Reflect and adjust

The final page asks what engaged students, which concepts needed another explanation, how students responded, and what should change next time. The reflection, supervisor remarks and signature fields are blank.

A completed reflection would connect one observed response to a specific teaching change. An anonymised response sheet and mentor feedback would allow a reviewer to assess that connection.
