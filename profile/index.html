My story

From studying history
to preparing to teach it.

Follow my teaching journey →

My interest in History grew from memorising events to asking why they happened, how we know, and what they mean to different people. Studying historical sources and accounts of Partition shaped my commitment to teach through evidence, inquiry and multiple perspectives.

Five years of UPSC preparation strengthened my engagement with Indian polity, governance and public affairs. I now bring that wider humanities perspective to teacher education and classroom practice.
