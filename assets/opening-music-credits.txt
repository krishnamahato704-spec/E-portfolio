Opening piano music

An original melody arranged for this portfolio, edited to 3.2 seconds.
No voice recording, vocals, background ambience or navigation effects.

Piano samples: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm.

given a small room reflection, reduced to 16% amplitude and faded in and out
