# Interactive atlas portfolio

9 November 2026. This version replaces the earlier small headline/photo opening with a complete graphic scene.

The first viewport pairs monumental serif typography with an original topographic field. Teacher and Forever Student modes visibly change the palette, geometry, typography and supporting text. The switch sits directly below the title. Pointer movement shifts the contour layers; reduced-motion and data-saving preferences keep them static. The decorative canvas stops drawing off screen and when the document is hidden. It has no role in navigation or access to facts.

Andreas's current opening was inspected as an actual screenshot: large italic type, a blue pixel-graphic perimeter and floating navigation. The Awwwards homepage screenshot showed monumental type, a large visual surface and a floating browse bar. The atlas interprets that scale and prominence through original cartographic geometry related to Social Science. No reference-site source code, graphics or proprietary fonts were copied.
