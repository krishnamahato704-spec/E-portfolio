# Private lesson case-study worksheet

This is an unfilled preparation aid. It is excluded from the public website and is not teaching evidence.

Use a lesson actually taught and its existing records. Record:

- Date, class, subject and learning objective.
- Activity used, time available and any adaptation actually made.
- One anonymised learner response, the feedback actually given and what that response showed.
- What you changed during or after the lesson, with the reason supported by that record.
- Your completed reflection and any mentor feedback you have permission to share.
- Source files, public/private copy locations and relevant publication-permission scope.

Keep missing items empty. Do not write expected responses as observed results, invent mentor quotations or claim improved learning without evidence. The published Democracy artifact remains Planning evidence until real teaching records support a different description.
