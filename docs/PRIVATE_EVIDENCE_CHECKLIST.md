# Preparing evidence from a taught lesson

This is a private working checklist, not a completed teaching record. Do not publish it as evidence or fill gaps with assumed outcomes.

For one lesson actually taught, record its date, class, subject, objective, planned activity and what happened. Select an anonymised response showing the learner's reasoning, add the feedback actually given and explain one teaching change supported by that response. Include the completed lesson reflection and mentor feedback only when available and authorised.

Before making a public copy:

- Confirm the school's rules and the relevant pupil/guardian publication permissions. Record the allowed audience, purpose, expiry or withdrawal conditions and who checked them.
- Remove pupil names, roll numbers, faces, school identifiers and embedded document metadata when they are not authorised or necessary. Check handwriting and context for indirect identification.
- Keep the original response, permission records and mentor notes in private storage. The existing Owner Studio private-source area requires owner authentication. Use a separate redacted public copy.
- Review PDF pages and image previews as well as captions. Anonymisation of a caption does not anonymise the file.
- Confirm the public artifact's publication status and source. Preserve Planning evidence until actual supporting records justify a different label.
